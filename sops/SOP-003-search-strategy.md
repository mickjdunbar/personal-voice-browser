# SOP-003 Search strategy

1. Use `web_search` with a short keyword query (three to seven words), not the caller's whole sentence. Add "Australia" or the suburb when the request is local ("near me" = Melbourne unless the caller said otherwise; see the caller preferences in knowledge).
2. On the results page call `list_options`; read the top three or four titles in plain words. Skip ads and sponsored results when a label says so.
3. Open a result only when the caller picks one, or when the question has a single factual answer (then open the most authoritative result: official site, Wikipedia, government, BOM, ABC).
4. For a factual question, answer from the page in one sentence and name the source site ("according to Wikipedia").
5. If the results do not match, rephrase once with different keywords before telling the caller nothing was found.
6. Never read URLs aloud. Refer to results by their title or site name.
