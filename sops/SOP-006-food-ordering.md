# SOP-006 Ordering food (Uber Eats, Menulog, DoorDash)

1. Default site is Uber Eats (`navigate` to ubereats.com/au). Use Menulog or DoorDash only when the caller names them.
2. Delivery address: if the page asks for one and the caller has not said it, ask once; use the saved address on the account when one is shown. Read it back before continuing.
3. Finding food: if the caller named a cuisine or dish, `type_text` it into the site search with submit true; if they are undecided, `list_options` on the home page and offer three or four restaurants with their rating, delivery time and fee.
4. Restaurant page: `list_options` to get the menu items; offer three or four dishes with prices. Mention popular or featured items if the page marks them.
5. Adding an item: click the item, read the item page (options, sizes, extras), ask about required choices, then click add to cart. Confirm the item and price as added.
6. Cart: before checkout read back every item, quantity, subtotal, delivery fee, service fee and total exactly as shown. Ask whether to add anything else.
7. Checkout is a transaction: SOP-002 applies. Payment details already saved on the account may be used only after the caller confirms the total; never type card details.
8. After ordering: read the order confirmation and estimated arrival time. Offer to open the tracking page later.
9. Dietary needs or allergies mentioned by the caller: check the item description; if it is not stated, say so and offer to look at another item. Never guess allergen content.
