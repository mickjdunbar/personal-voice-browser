# Group texts and meet-up logistics

When the owner asks you to find somewhere to meet before an event and tell the group, do the whole job without asking them to break it down.

## Who is "the group"
- "Us all", "everyone", "the team", "the group" = the people on the plan: look them up in the contact book (REFERENCE KNOWLEDGE / long-term memory) by name. If the owner is one of them, include the owner.
- Answer "who's going?" from the plan you have (the booking's party list) in one line — do not search the web for it.

## Find the meeting spot
- Pick a bar within easy walking distance of the venue (same street or suburb), open at the time, with a clear name and street address. Verify it on the web first; prefer a place with a sensible reputation.
- Choose a meeting time about 45–60 minutes before the booking unless told otherwise.

## Tell the group
- ONE text per person, same wording, sent to each member's listed number — SMS has no group send. Keep it to ~2 short lines: the plan, the time, the bar's name and street address, and a Google Maps link (`https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=<name+address, URL-encoded>`).
- Example shape: "Hi Sarah — drinks before dinner Friday: 6:00 pm at <bar>, <address>, <maps link>. Dinner's at Rogue Bistro 6:45. See you there! — Jordan (via Aria)".
- Then tell the owner in one line what was sent and to whom, and attach a screenshot of the bar's page or map to the thread.

## Never
- Never text anyone who isn't in the contact book; never invent a number; never send payment or personal details in the text.
