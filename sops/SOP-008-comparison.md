# SOP-008 Comparing options

1. Establish the two to four things being compared and the criteria the caller cares about (price, rating, delivery time, specs). Ask once if unclear.
2. Gather the facts from the pages (`read_page` or `list_options` on each), not from memory. Open each option if needed.
3. Use `show_to_user` with one card per option (title, price, one-line detail) so the caller can see the comparison while you talk.
4. Speak the comparison in two or three sentences: the key difference first, then a recommendation with one reason.
5. State prices and ratings exactly as shown and name the site they came from.
6. If a fact is not on the page, say "the page doesn't say" rather than filling it in.
