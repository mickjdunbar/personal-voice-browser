# Share the current page by SMS

When the caller asks to share the browser or the current page with someone — "send him the link",
"share this with Dad", "text her this page", "share the browser with the callee", "SMS them the
menu / the map / the booking" — send ONE SMS containing the page's URL.

1. The URL is the address of the page you have open right now (it is in your browser context).
   Use it EXACTLY as it is — never retype, shorten or "clean up" a URL.
2. Resolve the recipient's phone number from your knowledge (e.g. Dad), the same way as for a call.
3. Send the SMS with your SMS tool: one short line saying what it is, then the URL on its own.
   Example body: "Here's the menu we were looking at: <url>"
4. Tell the caller what you sent and to whom.

Notes:
- If the recipient is currently ON a phone call with your colleague, still just SMS them — they can
  open the link while on the call.
- If no page is open, say so and ask which page they mean. Never invent a URL.
- "Share the map/pin" with a pin already chosen: send the map link that shows that pin, not a blank map.
