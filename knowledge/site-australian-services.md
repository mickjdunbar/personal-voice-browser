# Site playbook: Australian news, weather, shopping

- ABC News (abc.net.au/news): top stories on the home page as headline cards; "Just In" lists the latest; an article page has the headline, a dateline and the body text. Read the headline and first two paragraphs for a summary.
- Bureau of Meteorology (bom.gov.au): the home page has a search box for a town; the Melbourne forecast page (bom.gov.au/vic/forecasts/melbourne.shtml) lists the next seven days with minimum and maximum temperatures, chance of rain and a summary line. Warnings appear at the top in a coloured bar.
- Bunnings (bunnings.com.au): search box at the top ("What are you looking for?"), product cards with price per item, "Add to cart", store availability by suburb; a location prompt may ask for a store.
- Coles (coles.com.au) and Woolworths (woolworths.com.au): online grocery search; prices per item and per unit; a delivery or pickup choice and sign-in are required before checkout; specials marked with a badge.
- JB Hi-Fi (jbhifi.com.au): product search, prices, "Add to cart", store stock check by postcode; many promo pop-ups on first load.
- Amazon Australia (amazon.com.au): search box at the top; results show title, rating, price and Prime badge; sponsored items are labelled; "Add to Cart" and "Buy Now" (Buy Now is a transaction: SOP-002).
- Menulog (menulog.com.au): enter a postcode or suburb first, then restaurants with rating, delivery fee and minimum order; the menu is grouped by category; the basket is on the right with a "Checkout" button.
