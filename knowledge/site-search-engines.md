# Site playbook: search engines and reference sites

- Bing (bing.com/search?q=) is the default search engine for the agent: results are a list with a title link, the site name above it and a snippet; ads are labelled "Ad"; "Next" at the bottom pages on. No sign-in or consent dialog in Australia.
- Google (google.com): may show a cookie consent dialog ("Accept all" / "Reject all") and a "Before you continue" page; results include an AI overview, "People also ask" and sponsored results marked "Sponsored". Google also shows an "unusual traffic" block to automated browsers; use Bing (web_search) instead.
- Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org): the search box is at the top ("Search Wikipedia"); an article has an infobox on the right (key facts: dates, population, founder) and a lead paragraph that summarises the subject; the table of contents lists sections. For a quick fact, read the first two paragraphs.
- DuckDuckGo (duckduckgo.com) refuses automated browsers with an "anomaly" page; do not use it for searches.
