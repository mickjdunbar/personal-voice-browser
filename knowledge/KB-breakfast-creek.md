# Breakfast Creek Hotel (the "Brekky Creek")

Iconic Brisbane pub, famous for its steaks (cuts on display at the counter) and "beer off the wood".

- Address: 2 Kingsford Smith Drive, Albion QLD 4010
- Map coordinates: lat -27.44076, lng 153.04565 (use as the centre for any map xApp about meeting here)
- Phone: (07) 3262 5988
- Website: https://www.breakfastcreekhotel.com

## Booking a table (the fast way - do this, not the hotel site)
The hotel site buries its booking widget in an iframe. Instead, `navigate` STRAIGHT to the
SevenRooms page WITH the booking pre-filled in the URL (all three params work):

`https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/breakfastcreekhotel/reservations/create/search?date=YYYY-MM-DD&party_size=N&start_time=HH:MM`

Example - 2 people, Friday 13 Nov 2026, 1pm:
`https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/breakfastcreekhotel/reservations/create/search?date=2026-11-13&party_size=2&start_time=13:00`

The page lands with Guests/Date/Time set and shows time-slot BUTTONS like "13:00 Beer Garden".
Click the slot matching the wanted time -> the CHECKOUT page.

## Checkout rules (SevenRooms holds the table for only ~3 minutes!)
- A countdown at the top (e.g. "3:12") is the SLOT HOLD. Work fast: fill First Name, Last Name,
  Email and Phone Number straight away from what you know about the caller (these are contact
  details, not credentials - you may type them; ask only for what you do not know). Skip Birthday
  and any optional fields. Use "continue as a guest" - never Log In.
- The final "Complete Reservation" / "Book" click is a transaction: SOP-002 - read the booking
  back and get one yes BEFORE you start filling, so the hold does not expire while you wait.
- If the hold EXPIRES or the final click bounces to an error / "not found" page: do not fiddle
  with the broken page. Go BACK to the pre-filled search URL above, click the slot again (a fresh
  hold), and resubmit - the second pass takes seconds.
- Success = a confirmation on screen with a booking reference. Read it to the caller. No visible
  confirmation = NOT booked; say so plainly and retry rather than guessing.

## Menus
Menus are at https://www.breakfastcreekhotel.com/menus (content loads a moment after the page renders -
`wait` 2 seconds then `look` if it seems empty). The steak section is the signature: rump cuts,
rib fillet and T-bone from the Staghorn Bistro / Beer Garden grill.

## Dining areas

- Spanish Garden - restaurant-style
