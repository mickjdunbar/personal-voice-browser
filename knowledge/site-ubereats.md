# Site playbook: Uber Eats Australia (ubereats.com/au)

- Home page: a delivery address box at the top ("Enter delivery address"), then rows of restaurants by category (Offers, Pizza, Burgers, Thai, Sushi, Indian, Healthy, Desserts). When signed in, the saved address is already selected and "Order again" shows past restaurants.
- Site search: the "Search Uber Eats" box at the top; type a dish, cuisine or restaurant name and press Enter. Results page lists restaurants with rating (out of 5), review count, delivery time range and delivery fee; a filter bar offers Pickup, Delivery, Offers, Under 30 min, Price, Dietary.
- Restaurant page: header with rating, delivery time, fee and opening hours; the menu is grouped by sections (Featured items, Picked for you, Mains, Sides, Drinks). Each item card shows the name, price and a short description; clicking opens an item dialog with required choices (size, base), optional extras, quantity and an "Add to order" or "Add 1 to order" button.
- Cart: the cart icon at the top right opens a side panel with items, subtotal and a "Go to checkout" button. Checkout page shows delivery address, delivery option (Priority, Standard, Schedule), payment method, the fee breakdown (subtotal, delivery fee, service fee, tip) and a "Place order" button. Place order is the transaction step (SOP-002).
- Uber One members see $0 delivery fee on eligible orders marked with the Uber One badge.
- After ordering: the order tracking page shows the status (preparing, picked up, arriving) and estimated arrival time; "Orders" in the account menu lists past orders with "Reorder".
- Common blockers: the address prompt appears before browsing when not signed in; a promo pop-up on first visit (close with the X); some restaurants are closed outside their hours and show "Currently unavailable".
